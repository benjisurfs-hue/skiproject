## Train and onward transfers

All five train entries require separate ground transportation. No through ticket, guaranteed connection, taxi availability, or mountain-direct train service is promised.

- **Killington and Pico:** [Amtrak Ethan Allen Express](https://www.amtrak.com/ethan-allen-express-train) serves New York and [Rutland (RUD)](https://www.amtrak.com/stations/rud). [The Bus Rutland–Killington Commuter](https://thebus.com/routes/rutland-killington-commuter/) continues to the resort area. The operator's [2026 schedule](https://thebus.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/2026-RKC-Winter-to-Summer.pdf) lists Pico Resort Hotel, K1 Base Lodge and Snowshed Base Lodge. Transfer timing must be checked for the actual travel date; train arrival and bus operation may not align. No departure times or fares are embedded in the resort data.
- **Stowe:** The resort's [Getting Here](https://www.stowe.com/explore-the-resort/about-the-resort/getting-here.aspx) identifies Waterbury station. [Amtrak Waterbury–Stowe (WAB)](https://www.amtrak.com/stations/wab) and the [Vermonter](https://www.amtrak.com/vermonter-train) are the rail references. [Blazer Taxi](https://www.taxistowevt.com/home.html) advertises Waterbury Amtrak pickup and Stowe mountain service. The UI advises arranging a taxi separately in advance, without promising a scheduled shuttle.
- **Bolton Valley:** [Getting Here](https://www.boltonvalley.com/plan-your-trip/getting-here/) identifies Waterbury–Stowe (WAB). [Blazer Taxi](https://www.taxistowevt.com/home.html) lists Waterbury Amtrak pickup and Bolton Valley in its ski-area service region. The UI advises arranging a taxi separately in advance. No timed public-transit connection was verified.
- **Mount Snow:** [Getting Here](https://www.mountsnow.com/explore-the-resort/about-the-resort/getting-here.aspx) identifies the Vermonter to Brattleboro and lists local car services. [Amtrak Brattleboro (BRA)](https://www.amtrak.com/stations/bra) is the rail link. The UI advises arranging a car service separately. [MOOver Route 10](https://www.moover.com/route/wilmington-brattleboro/) runs weekdays with holiday exceptions and would require further travel from Wilmington. It is not advertised as an assured train connection or a NYC ski bus.
